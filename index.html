Studying

Let's be completely honest, most student's do not like studying. It feels tiring, boring and overwhelming. Plus, we lack the motication.

Here are some tips to make it easier and more engaging!

Make your environment your own ✨
Having comfortable lighting, snacks, or even music in the background can help increase motivation and help you study.
Use colored sticky notes or highlighters to make your work look more pleasing, which increases motivation.
Turn it into a game by making it making quizzes

Study Smarter, Not Longer 🗓️
Use studying techniques like the Pomorodo Technique (Study for 25 minutes, take a 5 minute break).
Focus on understanding, not memorizing the content.

Don't study alone 🕺
joining study groups or pairing with a friend can be extremely helpful in studying! You can quiz each other on the content, or create a pretend competition on study streaks, or point!

Procrastination

Procrastination is an effortless thing to fall into. You want to study, but at the same time, you don't!

Here are some tips to minimize procrastination!

Break the I'll do it cycle 🔁
Doing anything, even doing one question, can prevent procrastination. Starting is always the hardest part - momentum kicks in once you get going!

Make it inconvenient
Make procrastinating difficult.You could put your phone across the room, or use website blockers that make it hard to do things other than your work!

Talk to yourself 🗣️
Saying positive things to yourself can overcome the mental fog for studying!

Last Tips

And finally, some Last Tips to make your studying adventure fun!

Set a deadline - even if it is fake 😦
Setting a timer or deadline can trick your brain into urgency.

Change your location 📍
Having a new environment can help refresh your mindset before you lock in!

Use a to-do list, but keep it short 📋
Using a to-do list with only three tasks can make it less overwhelming to study
